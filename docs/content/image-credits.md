# Placeholder photography: credits and licence

used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use and modify,

They stand in for Mise’s own photography and should be replaced before launch. Two
limits of stock photography to keep in mind: none of these dishes were cooked by Mise, and
the people shown have no connection to Mise.

Each image was resized to a 1,800 px long edge and re-encoded. The files live in
`apps/marketing/src/assets/images/`. When an image is replaced with Mise photography,
delete its row here and in `apps/marketing/src/content/image-credits.ts`, and rewrite its
alt text in `src/content`.
